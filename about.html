Who We Are
Rooted in Faith,
Reaching the World

GMJ Podcast — Glory, Majesty & Joy — began as a small recording in a church vestry. What started as a local ministry resource quickly grew into a global platform reaching thousands of listeners across 20+ nations.

We believe the Gospel should be accessible, relevant, and transformative. Every episode is crafted with prayer, purpose, and a deep commitment to Biblical truth — whether you are a seasoned believer or just beginning your journey with God.

GMJ is not just content — it is community, counsel, and a place where the lost find direction and the weary find rest.
